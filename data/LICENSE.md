# Data licence

The data.ftw database (everything under `data/`, and the published snapshots built from it) is made available
under the **Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0**. The full text is in [`LICENSE-DATA`](../LICENSE-DATA) and at
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/.

In short: you may use, copy, share and adapt the data for any purpose, including commercial use, provided that

- **you attribute it**: "Data from data.ftw (ForThisWorld), ODbL" with a link to https://github.com/forthisworld/data;
- **you share alike**: if you publish a changed or extended version of the database, you release that version under
  the ODbL too;
- **you keep it open**: you don't add technical restrictions (such as DRM) to a copy you distribute.

Works *produced* from the data (charts, articles, apps, analyses) don't have to be ODbL; they only need the
attribution.

## Source documents keep their own terms

Every fact cites its source. The documents themselves (gazette notifications, CAG reports, court orders, RTI replies,
company filings, news articles) and any archived copies of them belong to their publishers and keep their own terms.
The ODbL covers this database (the selection, structure, cleaning and linking of facts), not those documents.
